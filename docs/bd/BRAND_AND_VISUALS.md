# Field-kit visual rules

These rules apply to the outward diagrams and worked examples in this directory. They are taken from the
Wildcat brand guide supplied for this work.

## Start with the page

- Use a 16:9 canvas at 1920 x 1080 for screen material.
- Keep the page white or very pale unless it is a cover or section break.
- Use generous margins, one clear heading and no more than five main facts on a page.
- Put figures in pale rounded cards with thin rules. Do not bury them in decorative art.
- Use gradients for a hero band or one focal block, not as wallpaper behind data.

## Type

- Headings: Liberation Sans Bold.
- Body and labels: Inter. Use Liberation Sans when Inter is unavailable.
- Reference heading sizes: 60/80, 32/44 and 24/32.
- Reference body sizes: 32/44, 22/36 and 18/32.
- Use sentence case. Keep labels short enough to read on an ordinary laptop screen.

Blue carries senior and neutral information. Purple can connect or separate classes. Red carries realised
loss or permanent wind-down. Yellow is for delay, arrears and caution. Never use colour as the only way to
distinguish a state.

## Illustration

The illustration language is sparse black line work and a geometric cat mascot. Supporting art can add
pace or character, but it must not carry words, numbers, logos or the economic result. Generated art is
always checked against the reference guide before use.

## Finance rules

- Show dollars, percentages and dates as typeset text, never generated pixels.
- Label the senior target as a target, not yield or coupon.
- Label the seven-day withdrawal cycle as a batch period, not time to cash.
- Keep the platform fee beside the borrower cost calculation and state its basis.
- Show senior value priority separately from exit-request cash priority.
- Put the assumption beside every modelled recovery amount.
- Keep qualifications on the same page as the claim they limit.

## Delivery check

Every final image must be a readable 1920 x 1080 PNG, use the palette above, keep exact facts in the layout
layer and remain legible in a six-image contact sheet. Source renders, prompts and composition scripts are
working files and do not belong in the outward field kit.
