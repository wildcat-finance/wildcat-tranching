The TrancheFactory is registered at the protocol level (WildcatArchController) and deploys one senior/junior set per registered market. Tranching is a lender-side vault on top of an unchanged market; the borrower continues to see a single unitranche facility.

3 The model

  • Senior holds a priority claim that accrues at a target derived from the facility's base APR (the APR times a governance-set, timelocked senior share, capped at the APR), funded from realised yield and topped from junior down to a floor. It is a priority claim, not a solvency guarantee.
  • Junior is the leveraged first-loss residual: it earns the excess spread and absorbs losses to zero before senior is touched.
  • Subordination is a fixed floor (junior ≥ 20% of TVL, senior ≤ 4×), enforced by gating senior deposits and junior withdrawals.
  • Valuation is realised-only and oracle-free: NAV is held at a high-watermark while the market is delinquent, so unrealised penalty accrual is never booked.
  • Redemption is asynchronous, mirroring the market's batched withdrawal queue (request → execute → claim); partial proceeds are allocated senior-first, and while the market is delinquent or winding down junior may only draw settled cash beyond the entire senior obligation, so first-loss capital cannot exit ahead of senior during a slow-motion default. Exits during delinquency are sized at the live price, so frozen-mark appreciation stays in the pool. Recovery crediting is balance-derived with a permissionless sync(), so cash arriving outside the poke path is never stranded.
  • Default mirrors Wildcat ToU §6.2 on-chain (timeDelinquent ≥ delinquencyGracePeriod + 90 days), per-market configurable, with a Loan-Agreement override. On trigger the vault freezes deposits, halts senior accrual, and winds down senior-first.
  • Governance is immutable logic + a bounded, timelocked parameter set; pause halts deposits only and never blocks senior exits.
  • Compliance re-enforces the Wildcat sentinel per user (sanctioned redemptions route to escrow); junior is restricted to qualified providers.

4 Properties & verification

Invariants

seniorValue + juniorValue == realisedValue at all times · junior floors at zero before senior is impaired (first-loss) · senior is paid before junior on every settlement (priority).

  • Built on Solady's audited ERC20, ReentrancyGuard, and SafeTransferLib; share conversions round in favour of the pool.
  • Covered by a Foundry suite of 55 tests: unit/behaviour, property fuzzing, 128k-call stateful invariants (conservation, junior-first-loss, no over-distribution), and mainnet-fork tests that exercise deposit, valuation, and a redemption round-trip against the live v-wmtUSDC facility and its withdrawal queue. Review regressions are pinned in AuditPoC.t.sol, each stating the finding it covers.

Two independent three-pass agentic review cycles have been run against the code, with every actionable finding fixed and regression-pinned; no human audit engagement has been performed yet — one precedes any real capital. Independent review focuses on the credit-loss / redemption edge cases, the gas/DoS profile of the redemption-request array at scale, and ERC-4626 conformance of the view surface. A revised deployment and entry model (borrower-initiated ownerless factory, per-facility entry gates, USDC subscription with the controller as lender of record) is adopted and pending build; see the Design & Risk Specification, §IX.
