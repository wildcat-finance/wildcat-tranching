How the pieces connect assets solid · state reads dashed

The base Wildcat rail runs down the right: lenders fund the market, the market funds Six Seven Ltd, and the audited 4626 wrapper sits on the market token. The tranche layer attaches on the left — the controller is simply another v-abcUSDC holder, splitting one lender position into two seats. Numbers key the legend below.

  1. 1Senior deposits v-abcUSDC, controller mints sr-abcUSDC. Open to any non-sanctioned address today (that gap is F8).
  2. 2Junior deposits, mints jr-abcUSDC — whitelist-gated. The dashed chip is the proposed IEnterGate pair on both entry edges (entry only; burn and claim never call it).
  3. 3The on-ramp: lenders put USDC into the market, receive rebasing abcUSDC, wrap it to v-abcUSDC — the asset the tranche layer accepts.
  4. 4Exit request: requestRedeem burns tranche shares and redeems v-abcUSDC at the live price for market tokens.
  5. 5The controller queues those market tokens in the market’s 14-day withdrawal batch.
  6. 6USDC lands at batch expiry (pokeRecovery / permissionless sync); _allocate fills senior first — reserving the entire seniorOwed while distressed.
  7. 7claim() pays USDC out senior-first, FIFO within class. Sanctioned claimants are paid into a sentinel escrow instead.
  8. 8The wrapper holds the rebasing market token; scaleFactor conversion, interest only ever accrues upward.
  9. 9Six Seven Ltd draws USDC and repays plus 10.00% APR — and controls that APR, which senior’s target tracks at 80%.
  10. 10State reads, not asset flows: APR → senior target; isDelinquent → mark freeze; timeDelinquent → day-118 wind-down.
  11. 11The sentinel is re-checked per user at the tranche layer on every deposit, transfer, and claim.
  12. 12TrancheFactory, registered with the WildcatArchController, deploys one controller + token pair per registered market.

The structure is pinned both gates exactly binding

This is the finding that matters commercially. With junior sitting on the floor, both subordination gates return zero: the remaining 5,000,000 of capacity cannot be sold to senior, and junior cannot leave. Growth is gated entirely on first-loss capital arriving first.

So placing this facility is not "sell 5,000,000 of paper". It is source 1,000,000 of first-loss, then sell 4,000,000 of senior, in that order. Junior's 18.00% is unchanged throughout, because the ratio never moves. If senior-led placement is commercially necessary, minJuniorBips must be set lower at deployment — it is immutable.

Loss waterfall junior to zero before senior is touched

Each bar is the same 10,000,000 pool as it shrinks. Junior's slice vanishes first and completely; senior only starts losing once the pool has fallen past the 20% attachment point. Nothing here is a mark-to-market path — the contract books a loss only when cash actually comes back short.

Above the attachment point senior's loss is (L − 20%) / 80% of its principal. On a one-year view junior's 360,000 of carry absorbs a 3.60% pool loss before its principal is net down; senior's cushion plus its 640,000 coupon absorbs 26.4%.

The 14-day exit cycle two-week floor, no ceiling

Exits are asynchronous and run on the market's own batch clock. A request lands in whatever batch is currently open, so the wait to expiry is anywhere from 0 to 14 days — a holder cannot choose their batch. If the batch is under-funded the market pays pro-rata and the remainder rolls, so a stressed exit is a multiple of 14 days, re-testing senior priority on each roll.

The distress clock reserve ratio 0 · grace 28d · window 90d

With a zero reserve ratio the facility reads perfectly healthy until the first exit the borrower does not fund — there is no buffer to erode, so no early warning. From that first unfunded withdrawal, the clock to wind-down runs 28 + 90 = 118 days: 8.4 exit cycles. And because timeDelinquent decays on cure rather than resetting, a borrower who cures before day 28 never pays a penalty and never approaches day 118.

Where the mechanisms stand

The full Q1–Q16 decision set is built and tested. Five mechanisms moved beyond the design spec under two audit cycles, all of them sharpening senior priority. Six items are open: one blocks deployment outright, and F8 now has a concrete proposed resolution — Morpho Midnight's entry-gate interface, driven by Wildcat's existing role-provider stack via the secdemo-gateaccess bridge.

Assumptions. The full 10,000,000 of current supply is assumed to arrive through the tranche set, and junior is assumed to sit exactly on its 20% floor. A partially-tranched facility behaves identically on the tranched sleeve. Tranche parameters (seniorShareBips 8000, minJuniorBips 2000, defaultPenaltyWindow 90d) are proposals for this facility, not settings — minJuniorBips and defaultPenaltyWindow are immutable once deployed.

Caveats. Senior's "8.00%" is a target funded by priority, not a guarantee, and it tracks whatever APR Six Seven Ltd sets — on an open term there is no maturity at which senior recovers principal at the original rate. Accrual is update-driven and compounds with interaction frequency, so the realised senior rate sits slightly above target. Junior can be written to zero before senior loses anything. Recovery after a real default depends on off-chain legal enforcement.

Gates. The F8 resolution is built, adopting Morpho Midnight's IEnterGate verbatim: two optional gate pointers in Params, checked only where tranche exposure increases, address(0) = open, sanctions sentinel unchanged underneath. Exits are structurally ungateable — mint/burn bypass the transfer hook and claim pays USDC directly. One WildcatRoleProviderGate deployment can serve Midnight markets and tranche sets alike, and a borrower sealed-entity credential pinned to WILDCAT_DELINQ_90D_V1 latches recourse disclosure on the same day-118 predicate as wind-down.
